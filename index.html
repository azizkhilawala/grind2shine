The Big Picture

You're both coming in with real training history. Aziz, your EPG Elite Bootcamp sessions (right up to February 17th) built you a solid cardio and strength base. This plan takes that engine and channels it into something more targeted: structured progressive overload with dumbbells, precise nutrition using the EPG macro framework you already know, and a schedule that works as a couple at home.

The approach is simple: eat in a calorie deficit, hit protein every single day, and train progressively harder each week. Fat loss happens in the kitchen; muscle preservation and building happens in the gym. You can absolutely do both at the same time, especially given where you're both starting from.

For Aziz: the gynecomastia concern is real, and while training can't eliminate the tissue itself, building a strong upper chest and back dramatically changes how your chest looks and how shirts fit. This plan prioritizes posture work, upper chest emphasis, and back thickness. The fat loss component will also help significantly as overall body fat drops.

For Yakuta: chronic back and leg pain means we train around it, not through it. Every lower body exercise has a back-friendly modification built in. The arm and thigh concern is addressed directly through the upper-lower split, and with consistent protein and calorie management, toning follows naturally.

WORKING WITH YOUR BOHRI HALAL DIET

Mosque-Catered Meals

Mosque food is real, filling, and often protein-rich (meat curries, dal, rice). Here's how to navigate it:

  • Prioritize the protein portion (meat). Take double protein, half the rice.
  • Remove skin from chicken if served.
  • Limit gravy/sauce (high oil/fat) to a couple tablespoons.
  • Load up on any vegetable sides.
  • Before attending: have a protein shake so you're not starving and overeating.
  • Eat slowly. It takes 20 min to feel full.
Home Cooking Wins

Your spice collection is an unfair advantage. Flavor doesn't require calories.

  • Chicken breast marinated in yogurt + turmeric + cumin + garam masala = unbeatable.
  • Tilapia, shrimp, surmai and pomfret all count as lean protein.
  • Ground beef (lean) for keema or kebabs.
  • Egg whites (6) + 1 whole egg is a fast high-protein breakfast.
  • Dal (lentils) = underrated protein + fiber combo.
  • Cook proteins in bulk on Sunday. Rotate spice profiles all week.
Aziz — Foodie Strategy 🍽️

You love great food. Use that.

  • Different marinade every 2 days: teriyaki, garam masala, harissa yogurt, lemon herb.
  • High-protein sauces: Greek yogurt raita instead of heavy cream sauces.
  • If you go out: protein shake before leaving. Order protein + salad/veg. Skip the bread.
  • One intentional higher-calorie meal per week (not a binge, a plan) keeps adherence up.
  • Quest protein chips or Wilde chips for a "junk food" fix that doesn't wreck macros.
Yakuta — Sugar Craving Strategy 🍓

Sweet cravings are beatable without white-knuckling it.

  • Protein pudding: 0% Greek yogurt + protein powder + berries. Tastes like dessert.
  • Fruit (1 piece) as a planned snack satisfies sweet cravings with fiber.
  • Cinnamon in oatmeal hits sweet taste without sugar.
  • Frozen berries blended with protein shake = thick "milkshake" texture.
  • If craving is intense: have the fruit, not the cookie. Get ahead of it with protein at that meal.
  • Identify triggers (stress at work? After dinner?) and have a replacement ready.

PRE + POST WORKOUT NUTRITION

Pre-Workout (30–60 min before)
  • Option 1: 1 scoop protein + 1 banana
  • Option 2: Oats (½ cup dry) + protein powder mixed in
  • Option 3: 2 slices whole grain bread + 2 tbsp almond butter
  • Keep it light. You want fuel, not fullness.
  • Hydrate: 500ml water before starting
Post-Workout (within 60 min)
  • Option 1: Protein shake + fruit
  • Option 2: Egg whites scramble + rice
  • Option 3: Grilled chicken + sweet potato
  • This is your most important meal for muscle recovery.
  • Don't skip it. Get protein in within the hour.
Hydration — Non-Negotiable
  • 3–4 liters of water every day, per EPG protocol
  • Only zero-calorie drinks (water, black coffee, herbal tea)
  • Sparkling water if you miss carbonation
  • Start every morning with a full glass before anything else
  • Keep a marked water bottle to track intake
  • Dehydration = low energy and poor performance

07 / Rules
RULES FOR SUCCESS
Not motivational fluff. Actual operating rules for the next 12 weeks.
01
Protein First, Always
Every single meal starts with protein. If you hit your calorie target but miss protein, the day doesn't count. This is the one macro you cannot negotiate with.
02
Track for 2 Weeks Before Adjusting
Don't change your macros after 3 days of bad scale readings. Two full weeks of consistent tracking gives you actual data. One week is noise.
03
The Whiteboard Is Sacred
Write tomorrow's workout on the board tonight. Walk in knowing exactly what you're doing. The session starts better, ends faster, and delivers more results.
04
Never Skip Two Days in a Row
Miss one day, fine. Life happens. Never miss two. Skipping two consecutive days is how habits break. One day off is rest. Two days off is drift.
05
Eat Before You Go Out
Mosque meals, restaurants, family dinners: eat a protein snack before attending. Going somewhere hungry means you'll overeat. This is the EPG going-out hack, and it works.
06
Sunday is System Day
Grocery shopping and meal prep happens on Sunday. If proteins are marinated and portions are ready in the fridge, the entire week becomes easy. Skip this and the week becomes hard.
07
Heavier Every 2 Weeks
If the last two reps felt comfortable, add 2–5 lbs next session. Progressive overload is the engine of this plan. Lifting the same weight for 12 weeks builds nothing new.
08
Sleep Is Part of the Plan
7–8 hours minimum. Fat loss hormones, muscle recovery, energy, and cravings are all regulated during sleep. Cutting sleep to train more is the wrong trade.
09
Pain Is Information, Not a Badge
Yakuta: if something hurts (not burns, hurts) during a movement, stop and modify. There are always alternatives in this plan. Training through actual pain helps nobody.
10
Measure Everything That Matters
Scale weight, measurements, photos, strength numbers, energy, and how clothes fit. If the scale stalls for 2 weeks but your measurements are down and shirts fit better: you're winning. Don't just watch one number.
